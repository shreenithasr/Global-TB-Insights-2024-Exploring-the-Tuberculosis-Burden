# Power BI Dashboard

## Dashboard Overview

The **Global TB Insights 2024: Exploring the Tuberculosis Burden** dashboard was developed in **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze and visualize tuberculosis-related indicators for 2024.

The dashboard consists of three pages, each focusing on a different aspect of the tuberculosis burden.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Global Tuberculosis Overview

This page provides a high-level summary of the global tuberculosis situation in 2024.

It includes:

- Total TB Cases
- Total TB Deaths
- TB Treatment Coverage
- TB Incidence Rate
- TB Cases in Children
- HIV-Positive TB Cases
- HIV-Negative TB Death Rate
- TB New and Relapse Cases
- Distribution of Global Tuberculosis Cases
- Top 10 Countries by Estimated TB Cases
- Top 10 Countries by TB Deaths
- TB Incidence Rate by WHO Region
- Estimated TB Cases by World Bank Income Group

The page provides an overall view of the global TB burden and allows comparisons across countries, WHO regions, and income groups.

### 2. Country-Wise Analysis

This page focuses on country-level comparison of key tuberculosis indicators.

It includes:

- Estimated TB Cases
- TB Deaths
- Childhood TB Cases
- TB MDR/RR-TB Cases
- TB Treatment Coverage

A **Country** slicer is provided to interact with the analysis and explore the indicators for different countries.

### 3. Tuberculosis Insights

This page focuses on comparisons and relationships between different tuberculosis indicators.

It includes:

- TB Cases vs. TB Deaths by WHO Region
- TB Incidence Rate vs. Treatment Coverage by Country
- Top 10 Countries by Childhood TB Cases
- HIV-Positive TB Cases by Country
- Global Tuberculosis Cases by Country

The page also includes **Country, Region, and Income Group** slicers for interactive exploration of the data.

## Power BI Visualizations Used

The dashboard uses different Power BI visualizations to present the data effectively, including:

- **KPI Cards** – for key TB indicators
- **Bar Charts** – for country-level comparisons and rankings
- **Column Charts** – for regional comparisons
- **Donut Chart** – for distribution by World Bank income group
- **Map** – for geographical distribution of TB cases
- **Scatter Plot** – to examine the relationship between TB incidence rate and treatment coverage
- **Treemap** – to visualize the distribution of global TB cases by country
- **Slicers** – for interactive filtering by country, region, and income group

## Interactive Features

The dashboard allows users to:

- Filter the analysis by **Country**
- Filter by **WHO Region**
- Filter by **World Bank Income Group**
- Compare tuberculosis indicators across countries and regions
- Explore relationships between TB incidence and treatment coverage
- Examine the distribution of TB cases across different geographical and income-group categories

## Tool Used

**Microsoft Power BI**
